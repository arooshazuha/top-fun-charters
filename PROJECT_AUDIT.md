## FAQs (verbatim from live site + policy docs)
- **How many guests?** "Either up to six or up to 13. There are two different packages and pricing schedules due to requirement of crew and other considerations. While the boat is large enough to accommodate many more, six or 13 is the maximum legal number of passengers we are able to take."
- **How fast?** "Top Fun is a performance yacht and can reach speeds close to 40 mph, however excursions usually maintain a speed of around 10mph to keep guests comfortable and allow dolphins to come and play around the boat..."
- **Is this a fishing charter?** "We pretty much do everything but fishing. There is no live well, rod holders etc."
- **What should I bring?** "Bring whatever food and drinks you would like (no red wine please). Small coolers, ice etc."
- **What should I NOT bring / rules?** No more than your booked passenger count (legal limit, no exceptions). No spray sunscreen, no pets, no drugs, no smoking on the boat, no fishing equipment.
- **Cancellations:** Requests must be made in writing (email/text). "We will NOT acknowledge any verbal / over-the-phone request or voice mail."
- **Weather:** Bad weather, heavy winds and unforeseen mechanical issues may alter the itinerary; the captain makes the final call. "The Captain's word is the law aboard the ship."
